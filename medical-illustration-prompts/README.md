# Medical illustration prompts

This directory holds **medical illustration prompts only** — not engineering-slide prompts, SVG diagram copy, or generic image prompts.

They illustrate the play’s surgical story in a graphite/charcoal style that **evokes** a medical-school textbook plate without being one: atmospheric, narrative, anatomically sound, and faithful to the procedure beat on the page (right tools, right action, right stage of the repair). OR story plates keep captions out of the image; the stenosis cutaway is annotated with crisp typeset labels.
